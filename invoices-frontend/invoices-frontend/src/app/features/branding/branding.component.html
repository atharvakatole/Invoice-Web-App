How it works

We scan your PDF for common invoice labels — Invoice No, Due Date, Bill To, Subtotal, GST, Total, and the line-items table — and remember exactly where they appear. When you download or preview an invoice, we draw your live invoice data at those same positions on top of your uploaded PDF, so the background, branding, and visual style stay exactly as you designed it. Works best with a blank template (labels and table headers, but no filled-in sample data).
